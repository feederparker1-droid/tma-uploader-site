What Tetherloop is

Tetherloop is a browser game: a one-button zero-gravity climb. It runs entirely in your browser, or inside the Telegram app when opened as a Mini App. There is no game server, no account and no sign-up.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps your progress on your own device, in the browser's localStorage: best score, daily bests, dust balance, unlocked trails and skies, mission and streak progress, play statistics, settings, chosen language, the tutorial flag and the nickname you type for share cards. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes all of it. If storage is unavailable (for example in a private window), the game runs without saving and says so on the title screen.

When the game is opened inside Telegram, the same progress data is also written to Telegram CloudStorage, which Telegram ties to your Telegram account so that progress follows you across devices. When the game is played on CrazyGames and you are logged in to a CrazyGames account, the same data is also mirrored through the CrazyGames SDK's data store tied to that account. In both cases the game writes nothing beyond the progress data listed above, and the storage itself is operated by Telegram or CrazyGames under their own privacy policies.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no registration, no passwords.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking and no fingerprinting by the game itself.
  • No cookies set by the game.
  • No server of ours receives anything: scores, nicknames and progress never leave your device except through a link you choose to share.
  • No network requests of its own. The only scripts the game loads are the host portal's SDK when it runs on that portal, and Telegram's web-app script when it runs inside Telegram.

Third-party portals and ads

Tetherloop is distributed through third-party game portals: CrazyGames, Poki, GameDistribution, Y8 and itch.io. When you play on one of them, that portal's page and SDK control the surrounding experience. On ad-supported portals the portal's page or SDK shows advertising, including optional rewarded videos and interstitials between rounds, and processes data for that purpose, such as your IP address, device and browser information, advertising identifiers and your consent choices. That processing happens under the portal's own privacy policy, not ours: see the privacy policy of the portal you are playing on. The game sends the portal SDK only gameplay signals: play started or stopped, an ad was requested, and an occasional "happy moment" signal (for example a new best score) that portals use for their own ranking.

If you are logged in to a CrazyGames account, the game reads your CrazyGames username to prefill the share-card nickname. It is stored locally only.

Telegram

When opened as a Telegram Mini App, Telegram passes the game its standard launch data, which includes your Telegram user id, first name, last name, username and language code. The game uses the name to prefill your share-card nickname and the language code to pick the interface language. It sends this data nowhere; see Telegram's privacy policy for how Telegram handles it. Telegram CloudStorage is described above.

Share links and share cards

A share link contains only: the level seed, your score, your altitude, a nickname you choose (1–12 letters or digits) and a compressed record of your run's path. Nothing in it identifies you unless you put your own name in the nickname. Anyone who receives the link sees these values. The share card image is drawn on your device; sharing it uses your browser's or Telegram's share sheet.

Children

Tetherloop is not directed to children under 13. The game collects no personal data from anyone; third-party portals apply their own age rules.
